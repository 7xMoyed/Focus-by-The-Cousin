# Product Specification — Focus by The Cousin

## Overview
Focus by The Cousin is a platform that helps students and remote workers find the best cafes and study spots outside their homes.

## Target Users
- University students looking for quiet study spots
- Remote workers needing reliable Wi-Fi and power outlets
- Study groups seeking appropriate venues

## Core Rating Criteria
Each location is evaluated on:
- Noise level (quiet / moderate / loud)
- Seating comfort
- Power outlet availability
- Wi-Fi quality
- Table suitability (laptop + books)
- Restroom availability and cleanliness
- Parking accessibility
- Average price range
- Tolerance for long stays
- Suitability for solo study
- Suitability for group study
- Best days/times to visit
- Crowd level by time of day
- Proximity to universities
- Open now / hours

## Study Modes
- **Deep Focus** — Maximum quiet, minimal distractions
- **Group Study** — Comfortable for teams, good seating arrangements
- **Remote Work** — Reliable Wi-Fi, power outlets, professional atmosphere

## Initial Scope
- Al-Majmaah
- Limited area of Riyadh (around King Saud University and North Riyadh)

## Language
- Arabic first
- Structure ready for English (i18n-ready)

## Out of Scope (for now)
- Mobile app
- Payments / subscriptions
- User authentication (pending Supabase setup)
